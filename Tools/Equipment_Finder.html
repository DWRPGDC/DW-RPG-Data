Equipment Finder

Search and compare every piece of gear in Dawn Winery.

How to use

  1. Finder: Search by name and sort the results. Weapons can also be filtered by type, and both weapons and artifacts can be filtered by world level.
  2. Compare: Add several weapons or artifact sets to see their full stats side by side. Swipe to see more. Drag a card by its grip handle to reorder, or use Sort to order by a stat.
  3. Differentiator: Pick exactly two weapons or artifact sets to see which one wins on each stat, and by how much. Tap the VS button to swap sides.
  4. Toggle Dark mode in Settings any time.
  5. Turn on Remember selections in Settings to keep your Compare and Differentiator picks saved in this browser between visits (off by default).
  6. Turn on Remember last tab in Settings to reopen the tab you last had open (off by default).
