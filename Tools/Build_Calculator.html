    ❓ How to Use

    Building your character

    • Pick a weapon and artifact pieces from the panels on the page — click a slot to open the picker.
    • Enter rolled stats for each piece, and set your AR and Frostbearing Tree level to shape your final totals.
    • Final Stats updates live as you change anything.

    Using the Bag

    • Click 🎒 Save to Bag under a weapon or artifact panel to store it for later.
    • Open 🎒 Bag in the top bar to view saved items, sorted into tabs — including an All tab showing everything at once.
    • From the Bag, click ☆ to favorite an item (favorites float to the top of their tab), Equip to put it back on your character, ⧉ to duplicate it, or ✕ to remove it.
    • To add an item straight to the Bag without equipping it first, switch to a specific tab (Weapon, Flower, Plume, Sands, Goblet, or Circlet) and click ➕ Add New. Pick the item, set its stats, and click 🎒 Save to Bag.
    • Use the 🔍 Search bag box at the top of the Bag to find an item by name across every tab at once.

    Build Slots

    • Open 📋 Builds in the top bar to save your entire current build — weapon, artifacts, skills, AR, and Frostbearing Tree — under a name.
    • Load switches to that build, Rename renames it, and ✕ deletes it.
    • Build Slots capture everything, including your skill picks and AR/Frost level — not just gear.

    Undo

    • After resetting your build or removing something from the Bag or Build Slots, a brief Undo toast appears at the bottom of the screen — tap it to bring the item back.

    Settings

    • Dark Mode switches the app theme.
    • Save Build stores your current build in this browser, so it's there next time you visit.
    • Reset clears your current build back to empty — this does not touch anything saved in your Bag.
    • Backup lets you Export your Bag and Build Slots to a file, or Import one back in — handy for switching devices or keeping a backup.

    Expected Damage

    • Click 📊 Show Avg DPS under Final Stats to open the damage calculator.
    • What are you doing this turn? picks your action: a Basic Attack, casting Curse (costs your whole turn), Basic Attack + free Curse (if you have a Vigilance/Bash tier selected), Slash (Primordial Jade Winged-Spear only), or a catalyst spell (Chihayaburu, Fireball line, Meteor, Stormeye).
    • Spells and Slash replace your basic attack for the turn and never trigger the Vigilance/Bash follow-up.
    • Vigilance/Bash — pick your unlocked tier to add its follow-up hit onto a Basic Attack.
    • Modifiers: toggle Paladin to swap ATK and DV, set Current HP % if Staff of Homa is equipped (its low-HP multiplier applies automatically), pick a Sage tier for bonus damage vs elemental enemies (basic attacks only), and enter existing Curse stacks on the enemy (each stack multiplies the hit by 1.5x).
    • This ignores enemy DR/DV — it's your raw expected output, not damage after enemy defense.
